Beranda / Narrative Text

Materi 01

Narrative Text

Imaginative / Literary
Definisi: Teks yang menceritakan sebuah kisah (fiksi atau nyata) dengan tujuan menghibur pembaca (to entertain) dan seringkali mengandung pesan moral.

📌 Generic Structure

Orientation
Pengenalan tokoh, waktu, dan latar tempat cerita.
Complication
Konflik atau masalah yang dialami tokoh.
Resolution
Penyelesaian konflik — bisa happy ending atau sad ending.
Re-orientation
(Opsional) Penutup atau komentar akhir cerita.
Coda
(Opsional) Pesan moral dari cerita.

✏️ Language Features

Past tense Action verbs Time connectives (then, after, finally) Dialogue Descriptive adjectives 1st / 3rd person

💡 Tujuan Komunikatif

  • Menghibur pembaca melalui alur cerita yang menarik
  • Menyampaikan pesan moral secara implisit
  • Mengembangkan imajinasi dan empati pembaca

📝 Contoh Teks

The Honest Woodcutter

[Orientation] Once upon a time, a poor woodcutter lived near a deep forest. He worked hard every day to feed his family.

[Complication] One day, his only axe slipped from his hands and fell into the river. He cried desperately by the riverside, not knowing what to do without his only tool.

[Resolution] Suddenly, a river fairy appeared. She offered him a golden axe, then a silver one. Each time, the woodcutter honestly replied, "That is not mine." Impressed by his honesty, the fairy returned his iron axe and gave him the golden and silver axes as a reward.

[Coda] Honesty is always the best policy.

Beranda / Descriptive Text

Materi 02

Descriptive Text

Informative / Descriptive
Definisi: Teks yang menggambarkan sesuatu (orang, tempat, benda, atau hewan) secara spesifik dan detail sehingga pembaca dapat membayangkan objek yang dideskripsikan.

📌 Generic Structure

Identification
Memperkenalkan objek yang akan dideskripsikan.
Description
Penggambaran detail objek: fisik, sifat, keunikan, fungsi, dll.

⚡ Perbedaan dengan Report Text

  • Descriptive: Spesifik — satu objek tertentu. Contoh: "My cat, Mimi, is…"
  • Report: Umum — jenis/kelompok. Contoh: "Cats are…"

✏️ Language Features

Simple present tense Adjectives (beautiful, tall, unique) Relating verbs (is, are, has, have) Sensory details Specific noun phrases

📝 Contoh Teks

Borobudur Temple

[Identification] Borobudur is one of the most magnificent Buddhist temples in the world, located in Magelang, Central Java, Indonesia.

[Description] The temple has nine stacked platforms — six square and three circular — topped by a central dome. It is decorated with 2,672 relief panels and 504 Buddha statues. Every surface tells a story of Buddhist teachings, carved skillfully into ancient stone. The temple faces east, and at sunrise, the view from the top is absolutely breathtaking. Borobudur is truly a masterpiece of human architecture and spiritual devotion.

Beranda / Procedure Text

Materi 03

Procedure Text

Instructional
Definisi: Teks yang menjelaskan langkah-langkah cara melakukan sesuatu secara berurutan. Tujuannya adalah memberikan instruksi (how to do / make something).

📌 Generic Structure

Goal / Title
Tujuan atau hasil akhir yang ingin dicapai.
Materials
Bahan-bahan atau alat yang dibutuhkan (jika ada).
Steps
Langkah-langkah yang harus dilakukan secara berurutan.

💡 Jenis-jenis Procedure Text

  • Recipes — cara memasak makanan/minuman
  • Manual — cara menggunakan alat/mesin
  • Tips — cara melakukan sesuatu lebih baik
  • Experiments — prosedur ilmiah di laboratorium

✏️ Language Features

Imperative sentences (Mix, Boil, Add…) Sequence adverbs (first, then, next, finally) Simple present tense Action verbs Numbered steps Precise measurements

📝 Contoh Teks

How to Make a Cup of Tea

Materials: 1 tea bag, 200 ml hot water, 1 tsp sugar, a cup.

Steps:
1. First, boil water in a kettle until it reaches about 90–95°C.
2. Then, place a tea bag into a clean cup.
3. Next, carefully pour the hot water over the tea bag.
4. Let it steep for 3–5 minutes depending on how strong you like it.
5. Remove the tea bag and add sugar to taste.
6. Finally, stir gently and enjoy your tea while warm.

Beranda / Analytical Exposition Text

Materi 04

Analytical Exposition Text

Argumentative
Definisi: Teks yang bertujuan untuk meyakinkan pembaca bahwa suatu topik penting, dengan memberikan argumen-argumen pendukung beserta analisisnya.

📌 Generic Structure

Thesis
Pernyataan posisi atau pendapat penulis terhadap suatu isu.
Arguments
Argumen-argumen pendukung thesis (biasanya 2–3 argumen + elaborasi).
Reiteration
Penegasan kembali posisi penulis di akhir teks.

💡 Tips Menulis Exposition

  • Setiap argumen wajib disertai elaborasi/penjelasan
  • Gunakan data, fakta, atau contoh nyata untuk memperkuat argumen
  • Reiteration bukan sekadar mengulang — pertegas kesimpulan dengan kuat

✏️ Language Features

Simple present tense Causal conjunctions (because, therefore, thus) Modal verbs (should, must, need to) Evaluative language Connectives (firstly, furthermore, in conclusion) Passive voice

📝 Contoh Teks

Reading is Important for Students

[Thesis] Reading is one of the most important habits every student should develop in their academic life.

[Argument 1] Firstly, reading regularly improves vocabulary and language skills. Students who read widely are exposed to varied sentence structures and word choices, making them more confident communicators in both speaking and writing.

[Argument 2] Furthermore, reading broadens knowledge across many fields. It allows students to explore topics beyond the classroom, fostering critical and analytical thinking essential for solving complex problems.

[Reiteration] Therefore, reading is not merely a hobby — it is a fundamental skill that shapes intellectual growth and academic success. Students should be encouraged to read at least 30 minutes every day.

Beranda / Report Text

Materi 05

Report Text

Informative / Scientific
Definisi: Teks yang menyajikan informasi faktual dan ilmiah tentang fenomena alam, makhluk hidup, atau objek secara umum (bukan satu objek spesifik). Tujuannya adalah menginformasikan secara objektif.

📌 Generic Structure

General Classification
Pengenalan umum tentang topik yang akan dibahas.
Description
Detail tentang bagian-bagian, kebiasaan, perilaku, fungsi, habitat, dll.

⚡ Perbedaan Report vs Descriptive

  • Report: Umum — jenis/kelompok. "Dolphins are…"
  • Descriptive: Spesifik — satu objek. "My dolphin, Nemo, is…"

✏️ Language Features

Simple present tense General nouns (Dolphins, Volcanoes) Relating verbs (is, are, has) Technical vocabulary Objective — no personal opinion Passive voice

📝 Contoh Teks

Dolphins

[General Classification] Dolphins are marine mammals belonging to the family Delphinidae. They are among the most intelligent animals on Earth, closely related to whales and porpoises.

[Description] Dolphins have streamlined bodies that allow them to swim at speeds up to 60 km/h. They breathe through a blowhole on top of their heads. Using a system called echolocation, they emit sound waves to navigate and hunt for prey. Dolphins are highly social and live in groups called pods. They feed mainly on fish and squid. Their lifespan ranges from 20 to 60 years depending on the species.

Beranda / Recount Text

Materi 06

Recount Text

Personal / Historical
Definisi: Teks yang menceritakan kembali peristiwa atau pengalaman yang telah terjadi di masa lalu secara kronologis. Tujuannya adalah menginformasikan atau menghibur pembaca.

📌 Generic Structure

Orientation
Pengenalan siapa, kapan, dan di mana kejadian berlangsung.
Events
Urutan kejadian yang terjadi secara kronologis.
Re-orientation
Kesimpulan atau komentar/perasaan penulis tentang kejadian tersebut.

⚡ Perbedaan Recount vs Narrative

  • Recount: Kejadian nyata, tidak ada complication & resolution.
  • Narrative: Bisa fiksi, ada konflik (complication) dan penyelesaian (resolution).

💡 Jenis-jenis Recount Text

  • Personal Recount — pengalaman pribadi penulis
  • Factual Recount — laporan kejadian nyata (berita, laporan ilmiah)
  • Imaginative Recount — pengalaman imajiner/fiktif

✏️ Language Features

Simple past tense Sequence connectives (first, then, after that) Personal pronouns (I, we) Action verbs Specific time & place Descriptive language

📝 Contoh Teks

My Trip to Bali

[Orientation] Last school holiday, my family and I went to Bali for three days. We stayed at a small, cozy hotel near Kuta Beach.

[Events] On the first day, we visited Tanah Lot Temple in the afternoon and watched a breathtaking sunset over the Indian Ocean. The next morning, we drove to Ubud to see a traditional Kecak dance performance and the famous Tegalalang rice terraces. The scenery was absolutely stunning. On the last day, we enjoyed the beach, swam in the sea, and bought souvenirs for our friends back home.

[Re-orientation] It was one of the most amazing trips I had ever experienced. We returned home feeling refreshed and happy, and promised each other to visit Bali again someday.
